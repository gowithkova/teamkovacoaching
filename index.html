IS KOVA RIGHT FOR YOU?

Dedicated to the process.
Not interested in shortcuts.

You're in the right place if

  • You want intelligent, personalised programming — not a cookie-cutter plan
  • You're ready to earn your progress through consistent action
  • You want to understand the why behind your training
  • You're focused on long-term capability, not just quick fixes
  • You want coaching that works around your limitations

KOVA isn't for you if

  • You want a generic program pumped out in 5 minutes
  • You're chasing a 6-week transformation, not a system
  • You're not willing to show up consistently
  • You want to be told what to do without understanding why
  • You're looking for the most advanced exercises before you've earned them

My journey has taken me from professional kitchens to the Royal Australian Navy, through bodybuilding and strength sports, and ultimately into coaching with a passion for developing human performance.

Becoming a father at 22 reshaped my perspective on health, resilience and what it truly means to perform. It reinforced a philosophy that lasting progress is built through consistency, ownership and purposeful action — principles that became the foundation of KOVA.

My coaching is built on an evidence-informed approach to biomechanics, movement quality, strength development and hypertrophy.
